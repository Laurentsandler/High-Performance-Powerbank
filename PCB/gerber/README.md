# Gerber Files

Place your zipped Gerber export here (e.g. `Gerber_High_Performance_Powerbank_V2.zip`).

## Recommended Fabrication Parameters (e.g. JLCPCB):
- **PCB Type**: Standard FR-4
- **Layers**: 4
- **Dimensions**: Match your board outline
- **PCB Thickness**: 1.6 mm
- **Copper Weight**: 1 oz (outer) / 0.5 oz or 1 oz (inner)
- **Impedance Control**: No
- **Via Covering**: Tented
